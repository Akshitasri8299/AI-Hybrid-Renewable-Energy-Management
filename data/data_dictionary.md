# Microgrid Data Dictionary

## 1. timestamp
The date and time at which the measurement is recorded.
Unit: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM

## 2. solar_generation_kw
The amount of electricity generated by the solar system at that time.
Unit: kW

## 3. wind_generation_kw
The amount of electricity generated by the wind system at that time.
Unit: kW

## 4. load_kw
The amount of electricity being consumed by the connected system at that time.
Unit: kW

## 5. battery_soc_percent
The percentage of charge currently stored in the battery.
Unit: %

## 6. grid_power_kw
The amount of electricity being supplied by or taken from the external grid.
Unit: kW
